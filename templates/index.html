      ★ 03 LEARN

      Media literacy

      six checks before you believe — or share!

      01 🔍 Fake Portal Scam

      Source

      Who published it?

      Named outlet with an authentic "About Us" and registered domain.

      🚨 Real-World Case Study The Viral ₹5,000 Free Recharge Phish

      Viral WhatsApp links like pm-free-recharge.xyz claimed the government is giving free 5G data to all citizens. The page cloned official government colors to harvest user phone numbers and OTPs.

      🚩 Red Flags To Watch
      • Unusual domain extensions (.xyz, .tk, .click) instead of authentic .gov.in or established media.
      • Zero physical office address, no listed editor-in-chief, and no grievance officer details.
      • Typo-squatted domains mimicking top outlets (e.g. timesoflndia.co).
      ✅ Pro-Check Action Check official portals (pib.gov.in) or look up domain age on Whois before clicking.
      02 ⏱️ Recycled Panic

      Date

      Is it current?

      Publish date matches today's breaking news claim.

      🚨 Real-World Case Study Recycled 2020 Lockdown Notice Panic

      During recent seasonal weather alerts, a genuine 2020 government disaster circular was recirculated as "Breaking News Today", sparking widespread panic about sudden market and school closures.

      🚩 Red Flags To Watch
      • Dates cropped out, blurred, or missing from forwarded order copies.
      • Mentions government officials or committee heads who retired years ago.
      • Urgent buzzwords: "Emergency: Effective Tomorrow Morning!" without a calendar date.
      ✅ Pro-Check Action Reverse image search the order screenshot or filter search results by date (Past 24 hours).
      03 ✍️ Ghost Writer

      Author

      Who wrote it?

      A real journalist with verified previous reporting and byline.

      🚨 Real-World Case Study The Anonymous 'Swiss Doctor' Cancer Cure

      Viral blog posts attributed a "100% cure for cancer" to a "leading Swiss specialist". The article was unsigned, and the accompanying headshot was a stolen stock photo of a male model.

      🚩 Red Flags To Watch
      • Generic anonymous bylines: "Admin", "Staff Reporter", or "Health Insider".
      • Clicking author name leads to 404 or page with zero social presence or portfolio.
      • Author photo shows AI artifacts (asymmetrical glasses, distorted earlobes).
      ✅ Pro-Check Action Search "Author Name" journalist on Google or check Muck Rack / LinkedIn.
      04 🤖 AI Deepfakes

      Evidence

      What supports it?

      Primary documents, verified recordings, and scientific consensus.

      🚨 Real-World Case Study Deepfake Celebrity Investment Videos

      AI-generated deepfakes of high-profile industrial and sports celebrities appeared on Instagram reels, supposedly endorsing a secret automated cryptocurrency trading bot with guaranteed returns.

      🚩 Red Flags To Watch
      • Mouth movements slightly out-of-sync with speech; robotic vocal tone.
      • Unrealistic guarantees of 100% returns or instant wealth without risk.
      • Heavily blurred or pixelated edges around the speaker's face and collar.
      ✅ Pro-Check Action Check the person's verified official social media handles or company press room.
      05 📡 Viral Rumours

      Cross-check

      Who else reports it?

      A monumental national event is always covered by multiple news wires.

      🚨 Real-World Case Study "All ATMs Closing For 10 Days" Audio

      A viral 40-second voice note circulated on family groups claiming the Reserve Bank was shutting all ATMs nationwide for a week. Citizens rushed to withdraw cash before fact-checkers intervened.

      🚩 Red Flags To Watch
      • The claim exists exclusively as a forwarded audio clip or single fringe blog.
      • Major national news channels and news wires (PTI, ANI, Reuters) have zero reports.
      • Forwarded tag marked "Forwarded many times" accompanied by emotional urgency.
      ✅ Pro-Check Action Search the headline on Google News. If only 1 obscure blog reports it, it is likely false.
      06 🎯 Deceptive Clickbait

      Context

      Headline match?

      Read past rage-bait headlines to inspect the actual facts and context.

      🚨 Real-World Case Study The Cropped 8-Second Speech Video

      A 10-second video of an official was cut to sound like a threat: "We will demolish everything". The full uncut 3-minute video revealed they were explaining demolition of illegal encroachments following a court order.

      🚩 Red Flags To Watch
      • ALL-CAPS screaming headlines with excessive emojis (🚨🚨‼️).
      • Videos trimmed abruptly right before or after a controversial sentence.
      • The body text in the article completely contradicts the sensational headline.
      ✅ Pro-Check Action Always watch the uncut full press conference or read the full article before sharing.
